Quoted block times

Scenario tool only. The figure below is a known-charges subtotal, not a quote, not availability, and not a full trip price. Average quoted block time is not loaded: no file of Clipper’s own JetInsight historical quoted block hours has been retrieved. No other operator or flight-tracking source is used. An average quoted block time is not a flown time, not a billed time, and not a confirmed final billable time. Type a quoted block time for this scenario in each leg. Do not put flown or billed times in those fields. Brochure times, when shown, are not quoted block times. Repositioning is not added unless you add a leg.

Whether a repositioning leg applies, and whether miles are statute or nautical, is not published. Nothing here converts a map into miles or time.

That surcharge is published “up to crew rest limitations.” What counts as same-day, and whether the $1,000 replaces a repositioning fee, is not published.

The occupied-hour rate is $5,500 on every tier. The tier does not change that rate.

The brochure says “$500 per flight” for the 10-hour tier and includes pet travel for the 25- and 50-hour tiers. Per flight is not confirmed as per leg or per trip, so pets stay pending. This preview does not add $500 or $0.

The brochure limits this to “away from base.” The website does not. This preview never adds it on its own.
