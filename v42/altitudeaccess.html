Cancellation Terms

Non-peak Cancellation Terms

  • Within 72 hours of departure, 50% of trip price is non-refundable
  • Within 48 hours of departure, 100% of trip price is non-refundable
  • Outside of 72 hours, no penalty

Peak Period Cancellation Terms

  • Within 2 weeks of departure, 50% of the trip price is non-refundable
  • Within 72 hours of departure, 100% of trip price is non-refundable
  • Outside of 2 weeks, no penalty

Simple,
Transparent.

How it feels to use AltitudeAccess.

Limited membership

Enrollment is capped, so your aircraft is never double-booked or otherwise unavailable.

Preferences on file

Catering, stocking, pets, ground transport: recorded once, remembered every trip thereafter.

Guaranteed recovery

Clipper carries AOG trip recovery insurance. If an aircraft goes mechanical, a replacement is sourced.

Priority empty legs

Members receive first call on heavily discounted empty legs across the Clipper network.

Protected investment

All client funds are held in independent accounts. Deductions are made only upon trip completion.

Shareable with others

Family, colleagues, friends: your card serves whoever you plan to fly with.
