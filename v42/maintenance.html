Proven
Capabilities.

Supporting owners and operators throughout the ownership cycle.

Every aircraft we service reflects a commitment to precision and excellence. Our deeply experienced, factory-trained technician team prides itself in delivering work that consistently exceeds industry standards.

From scheduled inspections to complex avionics troubleshooting, our comprehensive maintenance capabilities cover the full lifecycle of your aircraft. We always apply the same attention to detail, no matter the scale of the task.

As a trusted partner to leading OEMs, including Textron, Piper, Epic, Cirrus, Williams, Continental and Garmin, we deliver manufacturer-level expertise with superior agility and responsiveness.

Our workmanship is defined by care, transparency and accountability.

Piston, turboprop
& jet fully supported

Factory-trained expert technicians bring years of multi-platform experience, with Clipper serving as a Service Center Partner to Textron, Piper, Epic, Cirrus, Williams, Garmin and Continental.

Avionics installation & troubleshooting

From troubleshooting to installation, we deliver exceptional build quality, value and after-sales support. Clipper Aviation is a proud installation and distribution partner to Garmin.

AOG &
remote support

Fully equipped vehicles and technicians ready to provide remote support across Southern California and beyond, we'll help you minimize disruption wherever you are.

Pitot-static & transponder testing

Expertly-trained and deeply experienced technicians available to provide reliable and timely pitot-static inspection and troubleshooting support. Please refer to our OpSpecs for supported aircraft.

Pre-purchase inspections

Facilitating dozens of aircraft transactions annually, we provide comprehensive pre-closing inspections for all supported aircraft types. On-staff DARs are available to support cross-border transactions.

Parts sales
& sourcing

Clipper is a well-established distributor of both factory-new and secondary parts. Allow our expert Parts Department to locate hard-to-find items, minimizing downtime and expense.
