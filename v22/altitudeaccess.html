A program that takes you places.

Exclusive Pricing and Benefits for Elite Travellers.

A preferred rate program from Clipper Aviation.

Private aviation should be straightforward. Transparent pricing. Capable aircraft. Personalized service. Access when you need it, without blackouts or fine print.

This was our goal in creating AltitudeAccess, Clipper Aviation's preferred rate program, built for travelers who value clarity and certainty as much as comfort.

Members commit to a set amount of flight hours at a fixed hourly rate, with client funds held in independent accounts and drawn down only as flights are completed. In return, they receive guaranteed availability, a single standard of service across our fleet and an operator that flies, maintains and stands behind its own aircraft.

How a trip works

Booked, flown and closed out, minus the surprises.

01

Book

Call, text or email your dedicated trip concierge. Seventy-two hours' notice ensures guaranteed aircraft availability. Trips inside 72 hours are confirmed when the schedule allows.

02

Confirm

An itinerary is returned within the hour, with crew, FBO, ground transport and catering pre-arranged. One conversation, one document.

03

Fly

Drive directly to the aircraft. The crew will be standing by ready for departure and to handle all luggage. Once on board, departure typically occurs within minutes.

04

Reconcile

Hours are deducted from your account at flight completion, never in advance. Any pass-throughs are fully itemized and disclosed before billing.

How to join

Simple to begin.

Joining AltitudeAccess is deliberately uncomplicated. A brief conversation establishes fit, followed by a short agreement documenting program terms. Funds are placed in an independent account, where they remain untouched until your flights are flown.

A conversation.

We meet, in person or by phone, to understand how you travel and whether the program is right for you. No sales pitch, no pressure, just an honest discussion.

A short agreement.

The contract covers one year and is a handful of pages in plain language. Full refunds without penalty are available on exit for hours not yet flown.

Funds secured.

Your membership amount is held in an independent account. Clipper draws only as flights are completed, never in advance.
