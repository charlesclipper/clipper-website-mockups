A preferred rate program from Clipper Aviation.

Private aviation should be straightforward. Transparent pricing. Capable aircraft. Personalized service. Access when you need it, without blackouts or fine print.

This was our goal in creating AltitudeAccess, Clipper Aviation's preferred rate program, built for travelers who value clarity and certainty as much as comfort.

Members commit to a set amount of flight hours at a fixed hourly rate, with client funds held in independent accounts and drawn down only as flights are completed. In return, they receive guaranteed availability, a single standard of service across our fleet and an operator that flies, maintains and stands behind its own aircraft.

Cancellation terms

Your rate never changes, with peak flying days subject only to a buyer cancellation window.

Non-peak

  • Within 72 hours of departure, 50% of trip price is non-refundable
  • Within 48 hours of departure, 100% of trip price is non-refundable
  • Outside of 72 hours, no penalty

Peak period

  • Within 2 weeks of departure, 50% of the trip price is non-refundable
  • Within 72 hours of departure, 100% of trip price is non-refundable

Membership

Simple
and transparent.

The program defines what you are buying. The membership defines how it feels to use it.

Limited membership

Enrollment is capped, so your aircraft is never double-booked or otherwise unavailable.

Preferences on file

Catering, stocking, pets, ground transport: recorded once, remembered every trip thereafter.

Guaranteed recovery

Clipper carries AOG trip recovery insurance. If an aircraft goes mechanical, a replacement is sourced.

Priority empty legs

Members receive first call on heavily discounted empty legs across the Clipper network.

Protected investment

All client funds are held in independent accounts. Deductions are made only upon trip completion.

Shareable with others

Family, colleagues, friends: your card serves whoever you plan to fly with.

How to join

Simple to begin.

Joining AltitudeAccess is deliberately uncomplicated. A brief conversation establishes fit, followed by a short agreement documenting program terms. Funds are placed in an independent account, where they remain untouched until your flights are flown.

A conversation.

We meet, in person or by phone, to understand how you travel and whether the program is right for you. No sales pitch, no pressure.

A short agreement.

The contract covers one year and is a handful of pages in plain language. Full refunds without penalty are available on exit for hours not yet flown.

Ready to Fly.

Your membership amount is held in an independent account. Clipper draws only as flights are completed, never in advance.
