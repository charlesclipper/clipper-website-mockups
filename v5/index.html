Fleet

King Air 350:
the machine to meet your mission

An unmatched pedigree, serving as the
industry's leading executive turboprop for over 60 years.

A benchmark against which all other aircraft in its class are measured, the King Air 350 blends industry-leading performance with passenger comforts superior to that of significantly larger platforms.

Offering a spacious, pressurized cabin, advanced avionics and enclosed lavatory, it offers jet-like capability with turboprop efficiency, delivering maximum value to our clients.

With a reputation earned over decades of service, the King Air 350 remains trusted by charter, corporate, and government operators worldwide.

  • Cabin

    • 8+1 seats
    • Double-club seating
    • Enclosed flushing rear lavatory

  • Baggage

    • 6 large suitcases
    • 8 golf bags
    • 71 ft³ rear hold, accessible in flight

  • Performance

    • 1,806 NM max range
    • 359 mph max cruise
    • 5,145 lb useful load
    • 3,300 ft takeoff distance

  • Interiors

    • Custom interiors by Elliott Aviation
    • Quilted leather seats, LED accent lighting
    • Power outlets at every seat
    • High-speed Wi-Fi standard
