Accessibility Statement

Clipper Aviation is committed to making our website usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We keep improving the experience for every visitor and apply the relevant accessibility standards as we go.

Conformance status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) set out how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities. We aim for WCAG 2.2 Level AA and monitor the site on an ongoing basis to keep it there.

Third-party content

Some parts of our website come from third parties, such as our online quote and booking provider, embedded maps and social media. We do not control that content, but we work with our providers to keep it accessible and welcome feedback about it.

Feedback

If something on our website is hard to use, please let us know. Email us at info@clipperaviation.com and someone from our team will get back to you within two business days.

Need assistance?

If you would rather speak with someone, call us at +1 (855) 463-5999 during regular business hours and we will gladly help with a quote, a booking or any question.
