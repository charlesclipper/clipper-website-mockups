The Case for AltitudeAccess®

Typical private aviation programs offer a package of compromises. Rate and availability. Aircraft and cost. Service and scale. AltitudeAccess® resolves these by making a single deliberate choice — one capable aircraft, flown by one experienced operator at one honest rate.

We grow our program carefully, with membership never outpacing fleet capacity.

The King Air 350 is the largest cabin-class turboprop in regular charter service. For Western U.S. trips that define most members’ travel — San Francisco, Aspen, Los Cabos, Sun Valley — it carries more passengers in greater comfort than comparable platforms, all at a materially lower hourly rate. For these missions, the jet category costs more and often delivers less.

Most programs source aircraft from a patchwork of managed operators. Cabin finish, crew training and service standards vary from tail to tail, often invisibly to the passenger until something wrong becomes apparent. AltitudeAccess® members fly an integrated fleet — same equipment, same crews — across every trip.

Fixed hourly pricing is unusual in this segment. Most programs publish headline rates that dissolve the moment weather shifts, demand peaks or a holiday weekend arrives. AltitudeAccess® holds to its rate — Tuesday in February, Wednesday before Thanksgiving, Super Bowl weekend, any day of the year. The budget you set in January is the budget you keep in December.

Why a Preferred Rate Program

Benefits without the burdens.

Aircraft ownership — full or fractional — suits a tiny population of flyers whose utilization and tolerance for operational complexity both run high.

For everyone else, the math tilts the other way for four main reasons.
