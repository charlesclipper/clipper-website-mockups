Cancellation Terms

Non-peak Cancellation Terms

  • Within 72 hours of departure, 50% of trip price is non-refundable
  • Within 48 hours of departure, 100% of trip price is non-refundable
  • Outside of 72 hours, no penalty

Peak Period Cancellation Terms

  • Within 2 weeks of departure, 50% of the trip price is non-refundable
  • Within 72 hours of departure, 100% of trip price is non-refundable
  • Outside of 2 weeks, no penalty

How
to Join.

A few short steps.

A brief conversation establishes fit, followed by a short agreement documenting program terms.

Funds are placed in an independent account, where they remain untouched until your flights are flown.

A conversation

We meet, in person or by phone, to understand how you travel and whether the program is right for you. No sales pitch, no pressure.

A short agreement

The contract covers one year and is a handful of pages in plain language. Full refunds without penalty are available on exit for hours not yet flown.

Ready to fly

Your membership amount is held in an independent account. Clipper draws only as flights are completed, never in advance.
