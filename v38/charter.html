King Air 350:
machine to meet your mission.

An unmatched pedigree as the industry's leading executive turboprop.

The benchmark against which all other aircraft in its class are measured, the King Air 350 blends industry-leading performance with passenger comforts superior to that of significantly larger platforms.

Offering a spacious, pressurized cabin, advanced avionics and enclosed lavatory, it marries jet-like capability with turboprop efficiency, delivering maximum value to our clients.

With a reputation earned over decades of service, the King Air 350 remains trusted by charter, corporate and government operators worldwide.
