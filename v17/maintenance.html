Piston, turboprop & jet fully supported

Our expert technicians receive OEM factory training and bring years of multi-platform experience, supporting Textron, Piper, Epic, Cirrus, Williams and Continental as trusted Service Center partners.

Avionics installation & troubleshooting

With OEM-trained technicians, Clipper Aviation provides expert support for all avionics needs. From troubleshooting to installation, we deliver exceptional build quality, value and after-sales support.

AOG & remote support

Fully equipped and ready to provide remote support across Southern California and beyond, our AOG vehicles and technicians will minimize disruption and ensure your aircraft is back in the air quickly.

Pitot-static & transponder testing

Expertly-trained and deeply experienced technicians available to provide reliable and timely pitot-static inspection and troubleshooting support. Please refer to our OpSpecs for supported aircraft.

Pre-purchase inspections

Facilitating dozens of aircraft transactions annually, we provide comprehensive pre-closing inspections for all supported aircraft types. On-staff DARs are available to support cross-border transactions.

Parts sales & sourcing

Clipper is an active and well-established distributor of both factory-new and secondary market parts. Allow our expert Parts Department to help locate hard-to-find items, minimizing downtime and expense.
