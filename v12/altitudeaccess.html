AltitudeAccess

Private aviation should be straightforward. Transparent pricing. Capable aircraft. Personalized service. Access when you need it, without blackouts or fine print.

This was our goal in creating AltitudeAccess, Clipper Aviation's preferred rate program, built for travelers who value clarity and certainty as much as comfort.

Members commit to a set amount of flight hours at a fixed hourly rate, with client funds held in independent accounts and drawn down only as flights are completed. In return, they receive guaranteed availability, a single standard of service across our fleet and an operator that flies, maintains and stands behind its own aircraft.

Fleet

One platform, limitless potential.

Single-type fleet, with all the benefits and no compromises.

Clipper flies one aircraft type, by design. The King Air 350 offers the largest cabin of any turboprop in regular charter service, rivaling many light and midsize jets.

Crew proficiency

Every Clipper pilot trains and flies on one aircraft type, with proficiency compounding. The captain on your flight has likely flown your exact aircraft countless times.

Maintenance depth

Our in-house maintenance division operates a factory-authorized Textron service center and holds decades of experience on the King Air platform. Parts are held ready in-stock.

Consistency

Clipper aircraft share the same exterior livery, interior finish and onboard amenities. The aircraft you boarded last month is the aircraft you board next week.

Seating

Eight fully articulating main-cabin seats, arranged in two facing clubs of four. Each seat reclines, swivels and tracks inboard. For parties of nine, the lavatory converts into an additional seat.

Lavatory

An enclosed, flushing lavatory with a solid door sits at the rear of the cabin, a detail uncommon in this class of aircraft.

Work & comfort

Every seat includes an extendable tray table and power outlets. LED track lighting, ambient floor lighting, individual climate controls and complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi.

Baggage

A 71-cubic-foot rear hold, accessible in flight, holds up to six large hard-shell suitcases or eight soft-shell golf bags. External wing lockers accommodate longer items such as skis and fishing rods.

Service

The journey

Service that frames the flight.

AltitudeAccess aspires to provide its members with much more than a means of transportation. Our commitment to you begins well in advance of departure and continues even after arrival at your destination.

A journey is defined by more than the time spent en route.

On the ground

Ground handling by leading FBO partners, including Jet Aviation, Signature and Atlantic. Members drive directly to and from the aircraft. Once seated, takeoff is typically 5–10 minutes away.

Concierge

A single Clipper team member is assigned as service coordinator for each member trip, with one point of contact (email, texting, Slack and WhatsApp).

In the air

Cabins are stocked with premium, organic snacks and beverages informed by our Southern California roots. Preferences are recorded and honored on each flight thereafter.

Celebrations

Birthdays, anniversaries, family occasions. The cabin is yours to decorate: flowers, balloons, themed presentations or a cake waiting onboard.

Safety, verified.

Above all else.

ARGUS credentials in recognition of operational excellence.

Clipper is ARGUS credentialed: independently audited across pilot qualifications, safety program management and maintenance practices. Every flight is crewed by two pilots, both type-rated to Pilot in Command standards.

Standards, maintained.

Every aircraft is maintained in our own FAA Part 145 repair station, a Textron authorized service center. Factory-trained technicians cover the fleet 24/7, with a hot-spare aircraft standing ready for flight recovery and to minimize any disruptions.

Pilot type ratings are conducted at CAE and FlightSafety International. Recurrent simulator training and FAA checkrides occur every six months, twice the FAA minimum.

How a trip works

Booked, flown and closed out, minus the surprises.

01

Book

Call, text or email your dedicated trip concierge. Seventy-two hours' notice ensures guaranteed aircraft availability. Trips inside 72 hours are confirmed when the schedule allows.

02

Confirm

An itinerary is returned within the hour, with crew, FBO, ground transport and catering pre-arranged. One conversation, one document.

03

Fly

Drive directly to the aircraft. The crew will be standing by ready for departure and to handle all luggage. Once on board, departure typically occurs within minutes.

04

Reconcile

Hours are deducted from your account at flight completion, never in advance. Any pass-throughs are fully itemized and disclosed before billing.

How to join

Simple to begin.

Joining AltitudeAccess is deliberately uncomplicated. A brief conversation establishes fit, followed by a short agreement documenting program terms. Funds are placed in an independent account, where they remain untouched until your flights are flown.

Private aviation at its best is dedicated work, done carefully by those with the highest standards.

A conversation.

We meet, in person or by phone, to understand how you travel and whether the program is right for you. No sales pitch, no pressure, just an honest discussion.

A short agreement.

The contract covers one year and is a handful of pages in plain language. Full refunds without penalty are available on exit for hours not yet flown.

Funds secured.

Your membership amount is held in an independent account. Clipper draws only as flights are completed, never in advance.
