Our
Story

Based at Los Angeles' iconic Van Nuys Airport, Clipper Aviation is guided by an unwavering commitment to delivering safety, operational excellence and superior service. We are a dedicated and client-centric organization.

Our private charter division is defined by the care, dedication and expertise of our people, who bring extensive operational knowledge rooted in decades of industry experience. Leveraging highly trained pilots, technicians and support staff, we ensure every journey reflects the highest standards of safety, reliability and personalized service.

Clipper Maintenance prioritizes delivering to our clients deep technical expertise, a safety-above-all-else mindset and commitment to meeting promised turnaround times. By leveraging a highly experienced team and offering specialized services including pitot-static and transponder testing, we ensure aircraft are consistently maintained to the highest standards of airworthiness and readiness.

Located on the Northwest portion of Van Nuys Airport, Clipper Aviation maintains an impressive multi-hangar footprint encompassing over 30,000 sq. ft.

Our maintenance division can accommodate large aircraft, including most common charter platforms. A capable in-house parts department holds inventory on-hand for commonly sourced components, with items that require ordering often arriving next-day.

We provide well-appointed crew rest and briefing areas, as well as client parking handled by our line service team.

For transient aircraft or those exploring longer-term storage options, we offer both tie-downs and hangars.
